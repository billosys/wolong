## What Worked

- The current Chengdu supervised CLI contract was stable enough for wolong to
  classify parser outcomes from final `PANDAPI_STATUS` fields without scraping
  diagnostic prose.
- A checked-in parser fixture executable kept CI honest: it proves wolong's
  locator, argv shape, runner invocation, and mapper on both platforms without
  pretending the sibling Chengdu checkout exists in CI.
- The first public facade stayed small. `wolong:validate/2` composes config,
  binary lookup, runner invocation, and parser-status mapping without pulling
  in grounder/engine pipeline concerns.
- The survey found a real contract limitation: syntax-invalid and
  reference-invalid HDDL are both exposed as `input_invalid` with no
  machine-readable subtype.
